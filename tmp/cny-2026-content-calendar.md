## Risk Mitigation & Backup Plans

### What If Blog Doesn't Rank?

**Backup Plan:**
- Increase paid social spend
- Focus on WeChat community distribution
- Email marketing intensity up 2x
- Influencer outreach (micro-influencers in Chinese-American community)

### What If Sales Are Slow?

**Backup Plan:**
- Limited-time microphone bundle discount
- Free shipping threshold lowering
- Extend deadline by 2-3 days (communicated as "extended by popular demand")
- Flash sale on January 12-13

### What If Inventory Issues?

**Backup Plan:**
- Pre-orders with CNY post-delivery
- Shift to 10S promotion if 10P+ sells out
- Accessory-only promotions (mics, remotes)
- Pivot messaging to "Setup your existing box with our guide"

### What If Negative Feedback?

**Backup Plan:**
- Rapid response team (within 1 hour)
- Concierge outreach to unhappy customers
- Document issues for product/service improvements
- Turn negatives into positives (show responsiveness)
