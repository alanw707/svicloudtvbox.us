## 0–30 Days — Foundation & First Sales

### Website & Tracking

- [ ] Confirm GA4 is installed and `purchase` events fire correctly on the order “Thank you” page.
- [ ] Verify Google Search Console is set up and key pages are indexed (home, `/product/svicloud-10p-plus/`, `/product/svicloud-10s/`, `/compare/`, `/zh/`).
- [ ] Install or verify Meta Pixel and Google Ads conversion tags are firing with value + currency.
- [ ] Add at least one GA4 exploration or dashboard that shows sessions, add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase events by channel.

### Offer, Copy & Conversion

- [ ] Finalize a one-sentence value proposition for US buyers (e.g., “Family streaming + karaoke in one simple, one-time purchase box.”).
- [ ] Clearly differentiate SviCloud 10P+ vs 10S in pricing cards and PDP bullets (who each is for, key specs, ideal use cases).
- [ ] Ensure PDPs show trust elements: US-based shipping details, warranty/returns summary, secure payment badges, and at least one real customer quote once available.
- [ ] Add a short FAQs section on each PDP covering legality, internet speed requirements, what’s included in the box, and how to reach support.

### Facebook Page Relaunch

- [ ] (Re)create the Facebook Page following `docs/marketing/facebook-promotion-plan.md` (name, bio, cover, About, policies).
- [ ] Publish at least 5 initial posts: 2 demo/setup videos, 1 FAQ explainer, 1 testimonial/social proof, 1 clear offer (“Buy now” with link).
- [ ] Join 3–5 relevant US-based groups (expat, karaoke, multilingual families, streaming) and begin answering questions helpfully (no spammy links).
- [ ] Add links to the Facebook Page from the site footer, email signature, order confirmation emails, and any packaging inserts.

### Content & SEO

- [ ] Publish 2 cornerstone blog posts for US buyers (e.g., based on `docs/blog/svicloud-tv-box-us-guide.md` and `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`), and make sure both are live on the site.
- [ ] For each cornerstone post, include clear CTAs linking to the SviCloud 10P+ and 10S PDPs.
- [ ] Add internal links from the homepage and PDPs to these 2 cornerstone posts so buyers can find deeper information.

### Email & Reviews

- [ ] Add basic email capture on-site (banner or exit-intent popup) offering either a small discount or a “Quick-start guide” in exchange for email.
- [ ] Set up a simple 2–3 email welcome sequence for new subscribers (brand intro → FAQs / setup tips → gentle reminder/offer).
- [ ] Configure a post-purchase email that sends a setup guide and asks for an honest review on the site or Google/Facebook once the box is delivered.

### Paid Traffic (Small Tests)

- [ ] Launch a small Google Search campaign focused on brand + high-intent buying keywords, capped at roughly $5–10 USD per day.
- [ ] Launch a basic Meta conversions campaign with 1–2 video creatives pointing to the best-converting PDP (start with 10P+ unless data suggests otherwise).
- [ ] Create a simple spreadsheet or Notion page logging daily spend, clicks, and sales by channel so you can see CAC and ROAS over time.

## 31–60 Days — Scale Content & Retargeting

### SEO & Content

- [ ] Publish 3–4 additional articles focused on comparisons, troubleshooting, and use cases (EN first; add zh-TW versions where possible), drawing from topics in `docs/marketing-growth-plan-svicloudtvbox-2025.md`.
- [ ] Add FAQ/HowTo schema to key guides and FAQ pages, following the implementation notes in `docs/seo-plan-svicloudtvbox-us.md`.
- [ ] Reach out to at least 3–5 small US-based YouTube or blog reviewers and offer a review unit or affiliate deal; track outreach status.

### Facebook & Social

- [ ] Establish a consistent posting cadence (aim for ~3 posts per week plus regular Stories/Reels).
- [ ] Identify 1–2 posts that perform well organically and test boosting them for engagement ($2–5/day).
- [ ] Test one social-proof creative (testimonial, unboxing clip, or review screenshot) in the main Meta campaign.

### Paid & Retargeting

- [ ] Add retargeting audiences in Meta (site visitors, add-to-cart, checkout, video viewers) and in Google Ads (remarketing lists where policy permits).
- [ ] Launch retargeting campaigns highlighting PDP benefits and a simple incentive (e.g., free US shipping, extended support, or a short-term bonus).
- [ ] Review Google Ads search terms weekly; add negative keywords and pause non-performing terms/ad groups that spend without conversions.

### Email & Retention

- [ ] Add an abandoned-cart flow (1–2 emails within ~48 hours) that reminds visitors of the items, answers common objections, and offers help.
- [ ] Update the post-purchase flow with a simple referral ask (e.g., “Share this with a friend who would love family streaming + karaoke”).

---

## 61–90 Days — Optimize & Build a Flywheel

### Performance & CRO

- [ ] Review GA4 and ad data monthly to identify which channels, campaigns, and landing pages convert best; write down 1–2 insights per month.
- [ ] Test at least one PDP variant (headline, hero image, or CTA placement) and compare conversion rate vs the original over at least 200+ visits.
- [ ] Improve site speed on home, PDPs, and `/compare/` using the performance recommendations in `docs/seo-plan-svicloudtvbox-us.md` (image compression, LiteSpeed tuning, etc.).

### Scaling Winners

- [ ] Gradually increase budget (in 20–30% steps) on paid campaigns that hit your target CAC; avoid big jumps that reset the learning phase.
- [ ] Launch at least one additional creative angle (e.g., “family karaoke night” versus “cord-cutting savings”) and compare results in Meta and Google.

### Social Proof & Community

- [ ] Reach at least 10 public reviews across your site, Google Business Profile (if used), and Facebook Page combined; surface the best quotes on PDPs.
- [ ] Decide whether to launch a small private “SVICLOUD Owners” community (e.g., Facebook Group) and seed initial members via post-purchase emails.

### Reporting & Habits

- [ ] Create a simple monthly marketing report (Google Sheet or Notion) tracking sessions, conversion rate, orders, revenue, ad spend, CAC, and ROAS by channel.
- [ ] Schedule a recurring monthly review to update this checklist (statuses + notes) and adjust the next month’s priorities based on what is actually working.
