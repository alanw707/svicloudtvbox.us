# SEO Audit — svicloudtvbox.us (2025-11-27)

## Scope & snapshot
- Source: live homepage HTML (mobile viewport) fetched 2025-11-27 post-deploy; Rank Math active; LiteSpeed cache in place; multiple pixels (FB, GA, AdSense, Jetpack, PYS).
- Checked robots.txt and Rank Math sitemaps (`/sitemap_index.xml`, `/post-sitemap.xml`, `/page-sitemap.xml`).
- Verified `SiteNavigationElement` nodes now render inside Rank Math JSON-LD via `hasPart` on the WebSite node.
- This is a heuristic audit (no full crawl/logs/PSI/GSC), focusing on structure, schema, on-page, and indexing signals.

## Executive summary
- Navigation schema is now present in the live HTML (5 items). Past drop-offs likely due to missing hook + cache; both fixed.
- Overall SEO posture is solid but performance and indexing hygiene need attention. Blog visibility risk: post-sitemap shows a small set; ensure all posts are indexed and in the sitemap.
- Mixed-language slugs (e.g., Compare page uses percent-encoded Chinese) are acceptable but should be canonicalized/redirected if you prefer English consistency.
- Page weight is high due to numerous third-party scripts; prioritize LCP/CLS cleanup and deferral.

- Technical foundation: **18/25**
- Structured data: **20/25**
- Content relevance/on-page: **12/20**

## Positives
- HTTPS, canonical present; hreflang set (`en-US`, `zh-Hant-US`, x-default).
- Rich schema coverage: Organization, WebSite, WebPage, Product, ItemList, FAQPage, SiteNavigationElement (home), SearchAction.
- Clear primary CTAs and internal links to shop, compare, and guides from the homepage.
- Robots.txt is clean; sitemap declared.
- Navigation schema now injected via Rank Math filter (primary path) and visible in source.

## Issues & risks
1) **Indexing coverage uncertainty (posts)** — `post-sitemap.xml` lists few entries (blog hub + some posts). Risk of missing posts or stale `lastmod`. Posts may be thin/duplicated titles. Needs GSC inspection and sitemap refresh.
2) **Mixed-language slug for Compare** — `/機型比較/` currently used. It is fine technically, but if English is the primary locale, use a single canonical slug with a 301 to avoid signal split.
3) **Performance bloat** — Multiple pixels (FB, GA, AdSense, Jetpack, PYS, Woo scripts). Likely LCP/CLS hit on mobile. Many inline scripts and external calls before main content.
4) **Cache sensitivity** — LiteSpeed cached HTML previously shipped without nav schema. Future schema changes must include cache purge (and CDN purge if any).
5) **Breadcrumb schema missing** — Helpful for sitelinks and clarity; Rank Math can emit BreadcrumbList.
6) **Content depth & duplication** — Recent posts look templated/truncated in titles. Risk of low-value content and repeated snippets. Needs unique H1/title/description and more body depth.
7) **Slug/canonical consistency across languages** — Ensure zh pages have zh titles/descriptions and mutual hreflang; avoid mixing Chinese slugs for English-intent content unless explicitly targeted.
8) **Ad/analytics load order** — AdSense and multiple GTM/GA snippets appear early; can delay rendering and cause layout shifts.
9) **Pagination/thin archives** — Not assessed fully; date/tag archives may be thin. Should be noindex if low value.

## Structured data review
- Present: Organization (with address, phone, priceRange), WebSite (with SearchAction), WebPage, Product (PDPs), ItemList (pricing section), FAQPage (home FAQ), SiteNavigationElement (5 items on homepage), ImageObject assets.
- Missing/Recommended: BreadcrumbList; ensure only homepage emits nav schema; keep IDs stable (`#website`, `#webpage`).
- Action: Run Rich Results Test (live) after each deploy; keep Rank Math filters active; avoid duplicate WebSite nodes outside Rank Math.

## On-page & content
- Titles/descriptions: localized and present. Check for uniqueness across posts; avoid truncation in teaser and title fields.
- Headings: clear H1/H2 structure on home. Ensure posts use unique H1 with supporting H2s.
- Copy: homepage strong; blog likely thin—expand to 800–1200 words with FAQs and internal links to shop/compare/guides.
- Alt text: present on key hero images. Keep optimizing media sizes.
- Internal links: good from home; add in-body links from posts to product/compare/guide pages and back.

## Internationalization
- Hreflang tags present (en-US, zh-Hant-US, x-default). Ensure zh pages have matching canonicals and localized meta. Avoid mixing languages in one URL’s primary intent.

## Technical & performance
- Third-party scripts: FB Pixel, GA/AdSense, Jetpack, PYS, Woo tracking — defer/async where possible; consider removing redundant tags.
- Images: WebP in use; ensure proper dimensions and lazy loading (already present). Keep hero prioritized.
- Caching: LiteSpeed; schema changes require purge. If using CDN, purge it too.
- Robots: clean; admin disallowed appropriately.

## Indexing & sitemaps
- Sitemaps present; `post-sitemap.xml` and `page-sitemap.xml` dated 2025-11-20. Verify all posts are included; update `lastmod` on publish. Ensure GSC sees them; resubmit after fixes.
- Robots allowlist OK; no unexpected disallows.
