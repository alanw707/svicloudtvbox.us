## Full warranty wording

The SVICloud 15P includes a **1-year limited hardware warranty** effective from delivery date, covering manufacturing defects in the box, remote, and included accessories under normal use. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, or unauthorized modification. Warranty service is handled entirely in the USA: contact support, we diagnose remotely (English or 中文), and if hardware is at fault we repair or replace with a prepaid U.S. shipping label.

## Full returns wording

Returns are accepted within **14 days of delivery**. The device must be in original condition with all accessories and packaging. Contact support for an RMA before shipping — returns without an RMA may be delayed. Refunds issue to the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection. Defective-on-arrival units receive a priority replacement from launch stock (we cover all shipping).
