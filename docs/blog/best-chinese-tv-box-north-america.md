North America is not one shipping or warranty jurisdiction. Confirm whether the seller serves your actual country and postal code. US terms should not be assumed to cover Canada or another destination on the same basis.

## What to check

Check total cost, currency, carrier service, import requirements where applicable and the return destination before paying. Compare service terms for the exact seller, not only the brand name.

For a US SVICLOUD purchase, the current product and store policy pages are the reference. Ask support before ordering if your destination or intended application is uncertain.
