A device lock, a Wi-Fi password and an application prompt are different issues. Identify the exact screen and app before following instructions. Never share your email, Wi-Fi or payment password with a stranger offering setup help.

## What to check

For content navigation, our support guidance starts with Yogurt TV and its regional sections. If a specific app asks for an access code, contact support with its name and a screenshot that excludes private account details.

Warranty coverage is not unlocked by an app password. Keep your order details for hardware support and use the published returns process if a return is required.
