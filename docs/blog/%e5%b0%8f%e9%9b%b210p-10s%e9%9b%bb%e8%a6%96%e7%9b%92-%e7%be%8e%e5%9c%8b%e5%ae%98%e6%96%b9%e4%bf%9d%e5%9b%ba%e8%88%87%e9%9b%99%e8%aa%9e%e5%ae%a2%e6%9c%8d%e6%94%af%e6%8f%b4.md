The 10P+ lists 4 GB / 64 GB, while the 10S lists 2 GB / 32 GB. Both should be evaluated against the room, applications and budget rather than assumed to need different content subscriptions. Check current product specifications before buying.

## What to check

Prepare an HDMI input, power outlet and reliable network. Keep packaging and accessories until setup is complete. If picture or sound fails, note whether the issue affects the entire system or one app before contacting support.

For an existing order, provide the order number and model through the store's contact channel. Warranty questions and return requests are different processes; consult the policy that applies to your case.
