Measure the network where the box will be installed. Compare wired access with available wireless coverage; the advertised router plan is not necessarily the speed at the television.

## What to check

Check the model's documented interfaces and your TV's HDMI inputs. Avoid promising zero buffering: service outages, source quality and network congestion remain outside the hardware specification.

Before buying, verify the seller's contact details, applicable return terms and stock information. Keep the written order details so support can identify the right model and transaction later.
