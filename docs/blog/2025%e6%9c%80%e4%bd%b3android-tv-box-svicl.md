Start with three needs: intended applications, the TV's connections and who will operate the remote. A specification list is useful only when it answers one of those needs.

## What to check

The 10S is the lower-memory/storage option at 2 GB / 32 GB; 10P+ and 15P list 4 GB / 64 GB. Check current pricing and platform differences before choosing. More storage does not guarantee a specific streaming service will work.

Keep room for updates and legitimate app downloads, and review seller support and returns. If a premium subscription service is essential, confirm device certification with that service before purchase.
