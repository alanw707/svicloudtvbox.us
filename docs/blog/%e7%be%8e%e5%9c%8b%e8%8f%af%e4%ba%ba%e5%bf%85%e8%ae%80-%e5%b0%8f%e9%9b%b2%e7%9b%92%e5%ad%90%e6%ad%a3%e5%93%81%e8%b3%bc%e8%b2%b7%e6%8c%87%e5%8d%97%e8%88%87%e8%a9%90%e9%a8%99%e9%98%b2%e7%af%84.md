Check the exact domain before entering payment details. A copied logo, low price or official label is not enough to verify the seller. Compare the business name and dealer evidence with the checkout and invoice.

## What to check

Use the seller's published support channel, not an unsolicited message asking for a separate payment. Keep the order confirmation and inspect the model and package on arrival.

If you suspect a mismatch, stop installation and contact the seller with the order reference and photos. Do not publish your full invoice, address or payment information in public forums to request help.
