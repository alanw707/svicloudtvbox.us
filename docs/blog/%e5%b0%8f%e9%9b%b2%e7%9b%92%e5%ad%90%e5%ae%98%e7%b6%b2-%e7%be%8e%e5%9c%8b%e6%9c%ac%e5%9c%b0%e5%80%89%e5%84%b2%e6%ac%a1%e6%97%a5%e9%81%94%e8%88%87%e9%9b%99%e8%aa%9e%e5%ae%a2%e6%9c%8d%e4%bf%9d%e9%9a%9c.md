Separate an official-looking name from verifiable seller details. Review the dealer certificate, business name and policy links before placing an order. This store's contact and return pages are the reference for support, not another seller's promises.

## What to check

Dispatch and delivery are different milestones. Check the order's actual shipping option and carrier tracking. A general US-stock description is not a guaranteed next-day arrival service.

For a time-sensitive gift, ask about the destination and current stock before ordering. Do not rely on an old article title to establish a delivery deadline.
