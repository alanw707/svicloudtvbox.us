# Yogurt TV Not Working on SVICLOUD? Fixes to Try First

If Yogurt TV will not open, keeps buffering, shows a black screen, or does not load normally on your SVICLOUD TV Box, do not assume the box is broken. Most app problems are caused by network, cache, update, or setup issues.

Use this checklist before replacing the device.

## 1. Restart the Box and Router

Turn off the SVICLOUD box, unplug power for one minute, then restart your router. After your Wi-Fi is fully back online, reconnect the box and test again.

If the box is far from the router, try Ethernet or move the box closer to the Wi-Fi source.

## 2. Confirm the Internet Is Actually Working

Wi-Fi can show "connected" even when video apps cannot reach the internet properly. Test another app or browser page. If multiple apps fail, the problem is likely network-related.

Use the troubleshooting page for deeper Wi-Fi steps:

[SVICLOUD troubleshooting guide](https://svicloudtvbox.us/guides-troubleshooting/)

## 3. Clear App Cache

Open Android settings, find the app list, choose Yogurt TV, then clear cache. Reopen the app and test again.

Avoid clearing app data unless support asks you to, because that can remove saved settings.

## 4. Check App Installation Steps

If the app was recently installed or updated, confirm the setup path. Use the official app guide:

[SVICLOUD app installation guide](https://svicloudtvbox.us/guides-apps/)

## 5. Check Remote and Input Issues

Sometimes the app is working but the remote is not responding or the TV input is wrong. Try:

- Replacing remote batteries.
- Re-pairing the Bluetooth remote.
- Confirming the TV is on the correct HDMI input.
- Rebooting the box after pairing.

## 6. When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- Yogurt TV still will not open after restart and cache steps.
- The box is stuck on a logo or recovery screen.
- The same problem happens across multiple apps.
- You see a repeated error message.
- You recently changed internet service or router equipment.

Include your order number, device model, app name, photos or screenshots, and what you already tried.

[Contact SVICLOUD support](https://svicloudtvbox.us/contact/)
