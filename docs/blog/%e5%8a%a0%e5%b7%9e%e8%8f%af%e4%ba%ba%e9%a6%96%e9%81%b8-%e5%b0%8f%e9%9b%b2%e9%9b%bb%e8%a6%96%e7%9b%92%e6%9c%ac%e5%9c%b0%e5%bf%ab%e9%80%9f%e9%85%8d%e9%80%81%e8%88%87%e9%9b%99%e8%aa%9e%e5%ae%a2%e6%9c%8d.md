A California delivery address does not mean an order ships from a California warehouse. Our storefront describes Nevada fulfillment. Use the shipping policy and checkout for the applicable service; this article does not promise next-day delivery.

## What to check

Verify apartment numbers, postal code and the recipient name before payment. Carrier transit, weekends and holidays can affect arrival. Tracking is the right reference after dispatch.

For setup, check the network where the TV sits rather than only near the router. If you need Chinese-language help, use the store's contact channel and describe the model and error. Local pickup or an on-site installation should only be assumed after a specific arrangement is confirmed.
