Service comparisons should name the seller and exact model. One seller's local dispatch or warranty does not describe every unit sold under a brand. Request written terms from both stores.

## What to check

Compare order processing, carrier service, return destination, eligibility and support availability separately. A 24-hour dispatch claim is not a 24-hour delivery promise, and this article offers neither guarantee.

For SVICLOUD, use current store policies and model pages. For Unblock, ask its seller for the same information. Prefer clear answers over a broad claim that one brand always has better after-sales service.
