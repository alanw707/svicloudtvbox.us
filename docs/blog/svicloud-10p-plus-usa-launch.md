The 10P+ remains an option alongside 10S and 15P. Compare the current product pages rather than treating an older launch announcement as today's stock or promotion. The 10P+ lists Android 12 and 4 GB / 64 GB; 15P lists Android 14 and 4 GB / 64 GB.

## What to check

Before ordering, check your TV's HDMI connection, available network and intended applications. Confirm the cart has the desired model and quantity. A second room needs its own planning for network coverage and accounts, not just another box.

Use the store's setup guides after delivery. If an existing device only has an app or network issue, troubleshoot that first rather than assuming a replacement is necessary.
