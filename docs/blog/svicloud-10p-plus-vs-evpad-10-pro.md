The EVPAD 10 Pro is not the newer EVPAD 12P. Verify the exact model before comparing advertisements. For SVICLOUD 10P+, the current product page is the reference for Android 12, 4 GB / 64 GB and included accessories.

## What to check

For family use, test whether the navigation language, remote and setup process suit the person who will use the TV. Ask the EVPAD seller to document its own app, hardware and service claims. We do not have controlled testing that establishes a performance or reliability winner.

Check warranty handling and returns with each seller. If you are shopping current platforms rather than these specific models, compare the in-stock 15P too. Buying a newer box is not a guaranteed solution to an app-server outage.
