### Quick Connection Problem Diagnosis

**Symptom: Unable to connect to Wi-Fi or unstable network**

The most common issue with SVICLOUD TV boxes is network connectivity. According to our customer service statistics, approximately 60% of user inquiries are Wi-Fi related.

**Immediate Check Items:**
- Is router distance over 10 meters
- 2.4GHz vs 5GHz band selection
- DNS settings correct (recommend 8.8.8.8)
- Network speed reaches 25Mbps or above

**Real Case:**
Mr. Zhang from Los Angeles reported frequent disconnections with his SVICLOUD box. Our remote diagnosis found he was using default router DNS. After switching to Google DNS, the problem was immediately resolved. The entire process took only 15 minutes, no need to send back for repair.

### System Performance Optimization Check

**Symptom: Slow boot, long app loading times, screen lag**

Performance issues usually stem from insufficient storage space or too many background programs.

**Optimization Steps:**
1. Clear cache: Settings → Storage → Clear Cache
2. Turn off auto-updates: Avoid background downloads consuming bandwidth
3. Remove unused apps: Maintain at least 2GB available space
4. Restart schedule: Scheduled restart once weekly

**Data Support:**
Testing by our technical team shows regular cache clearing can improve system response speed by 40%, reducing boot time from 45 seconds to 25 seconds.
