Check microphone compatibility with the seller or microphone manual before purchasing accessories. Pair and test each microphone, then set volume gradually to avoid feedback. Keep microphones away from speakers.

## What to check

Try the intended karaoke app and an available song before the gathering. App catalogues and availability can change; a hardware purchase is not a guarantee of a particular song. Keep a backup activity ready rather than updating or resetting the box during the party.

Test the TV or sound system's audio path and note any delay. Change one setting at a time. For children's use, review supervision and volume, and keep small accessories out of reach.
