First identify whether the TV has no signal, the box cannot reach the internet, or only one app fails. For no signal, verify the selected HDMI input and cable. For a network issue, compare another device on the same connection.

## What to check

Restart the box normally, verify date and time, and retry the affected app. If one app alone fails, record its name and error text. Avoid a factory reset until support has assessed the situation; it may remove settings and apps.

Contact support with the model and a photo of the error. Use legitimate application sources. A server-side outage is not proof that the hardware needs replacement.
