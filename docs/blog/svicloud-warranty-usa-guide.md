# SVICLOUD Warranty in the USA: What Buyers Should Check

Warranty questions usually show up at two moments: right before purchase, and right after a setup issue. This guide helps U.S. buyers understand what to check before ordering and what to prepare if support is needed.

## Before You Buy

Check the official pages first:

- [Shipping policy](https://svicloudtvbox.us/shipping-policy/)
- [Return policy](https://svicloudtvbox.us/return-policy/)
- [Contact support](https://svicloudtvbox.us/contact/)

Policy pages are the source of truth. Avoid relying on screenshots, old forum posts, or third-party claims.

## What to Save After Ordering

Keep:

- Order number.
- Email receipt.
- Product model.
- Shipping tracking.
- Photos of packaging and accessories.

This makes support faster if you need help later.

## Troubleshooting Before Warranty

Many issues are setup-related, not hardware defects. Before opening a warranty request, check:

- Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection.
- HDMI input and cable.
- Remote batteries and pairing.
- App cache or app updates.
- Power restart.

Use the support hub:

[SVICLOUD troubleshooting guide](https://svicloudtvbox.us/guides-troubleshooting/)

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- The box will not power on.
- The device remains stuck after restart steps.
- Multiple apps fail after network troubleshooting.
- You received the wrong item.
- The package arrived damaged.

Include your order number, model, photos or screenshots, and the steps you already tried.

[Contact SVICLOUD support](https://svicloudtvbox.us/contact/)
