## What to check

Ask both sellers about included accessories, supported connections, app setup, warranty service location and return shipping. Local support depends on the seller, not only the brand. Avoid blanket claims that every competing box requires overseas service.

Existing UBOX owners should weigh familiarity and account migration against any specific feature they need. New buyers can also compare the 15P's Android 14 platform. Choose from documented needs, current prices and written service terms rather than unverified channel counts.
