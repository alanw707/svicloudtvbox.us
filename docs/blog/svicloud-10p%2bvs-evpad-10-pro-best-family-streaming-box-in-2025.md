Before comparing these models, list the applications, languages and TV connections your family needs. Ask each seller to confirm those requirements for the exact model being offered, not a newer generation.

## What to check

A useful demonstration should show menu navigation, app launch and remote operation. Ask whether the demonstration uses the same firmware and accessories as the shipped unit. We are not claiming that either model has been independently benchmarked here.

Compare the current 10P+ page and the detailed model comparison linked below. If your requirements have changed, include 15P in the shortlist rather than relying on a 2025 recommendation.
