Compare specific supported formats, not only a 4K badge. HDR playback requires compatible content and a compatible TV/input configuration. Codec support and the app's own restrictions also matter.

## What to check

Wi-Fi 6 capability alone does not guarantee a fast link: router support, placement and interference affect results. Test at the TV location and consider Ethernet when available.

Review memory, storage, remote controls and the actual applications you intend to use. The 15P, 10P+ and 10S have different configurations; use their current pages rather than an old best-of ranking.
