A search title is not proof of dealer authorization. Check the certificate's company name, territory and validity against the business taking payment. Our storefront displays its SVI.STUDIO certificate for 168 Media Group LLC.

## What to check

Confirm the domain in your browser, the model in your cart and the support address in the store's contact page. Save your invoice and do not send payment to an unrelated account merely because someone uses the brand name.

A serial number alone does not establish the seller's return policy. Ask the seller to state service arrangements in writing, especially when comparing marketplace listings.
