The practical published difference is 4 GB / 64 GB for 10P+ versus 2 GB / 32 GB for 10S. Compare the current product pages for all other specifications and included accessories. Both remain separate options from the newer 15P.

## What to check

For a secondary TV, identify the actual apps and storage needs before paying for more capacity. For a main TV, consider the remote and setup workflow as well as capacity. We do not claim a measured multitasking or playback improvement without testing.

If your problem is poor Wi-Fi coverage in one room, address that separately. Changing the model alone may not fix it.
