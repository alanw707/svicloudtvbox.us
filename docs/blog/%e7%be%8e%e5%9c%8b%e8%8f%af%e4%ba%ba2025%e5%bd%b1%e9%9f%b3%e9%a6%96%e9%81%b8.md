Set up the device for the person using it, not only the person installing it. Agree on menu language, subtitle preferences and where frequently used apps belong. Test the remote with family members before finishing setup.

## What to check

Use Yogurt TV as the first support-guided starting point for its Korean, Japanese, USA and Chinese sections, without assuming a particular programme is guaranteed. Review children's app and account settings separately.

Write a short household guide for input selection, volume and safe restart. Keep purchase and support details accessible, but never write account passwords on a shared TV instruction sheet.
