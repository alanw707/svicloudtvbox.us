## Customer Insights

### Customer Behavior Patterns

Streaming is mainstream in the U.S. (83% of adults use streaming; only 36% subscribe to cable/satellite). The heaviest streaming usage skews toward adults under 50, but streaming is still common among older adults. Streaming is common across income groups, with higher-income adults most likely to stream. _Source: https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/01/83-of-us-adults-use-streaming-services-far-fewer-subscribe-to-cable-or-satellite-tv/_

Households hold roughly 3.8 streaming subscriptions on average, indicating multi-service usage and ongoing subscription management. _Source: https://www.simon-kucher.com/en/insights/us-results-2025-global-streaming-study-0_

### Pain Points and Challenges

Content discovery is a major pain point; nearly half of streamers are willing to cancel if they cannot find something to watch, and viewers spend around 14 minutes searching for content. _Source: https://gracenote.com/newsroom/new-gracenote-report-highlights-impact-of-ineffective-content-discovery-on-consumer-happiness-with-streaming/_

Streaming performance problems are a hard stop; around three-quarters of viewers abandon within four minutes of poor streaming, and 40% report reduced affinity after one bad experience. _Source: https://telecompetitor.com/ott-video-satisfaction-survey-streaming-problems-drive-75-away/_

Price remains a core churn driver; one-third of streaming cancellations are attributed to price. _Source: https://cordcutting.com/research/state-of-streaming-report_

### Decision-Making Processes

Consumers prioritize cost, content, and convenience when choosing streaming providers. _Source: https://cordcutting.com/research/state-of-streaming-report_

Lack of interesting content (44%) and price (33%) are the leading reasons for canceling a subscription. _Source: https://cordcutting.com/research/state-of-streaming-report_

Plan flexibility and ad-supported tiers are now table stakes in a mature streaming market. _Source: https://www.simon-kucher.com/en/insights/us-results-2025-global-streaming-study-0_
