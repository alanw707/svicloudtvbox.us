## Choosing a model
- English answer: Choose SVICLOUD 10P+ if the caller wants voice search and karaoke. Choose SVICLOUD 10S if the caller wants the same streaming catalog without Karaoke KTV or the microphone-ready remote.
- Mandarin cue: 若需要語音搜尋和卡拉 OK，選 10P+；若只要一般觀看，可選 10S。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`

## Using the box outside Asia
- English answer: Yes. The box is designed for international use as long as the caller has stable broadband or Wi-Fi.
- Mandarin cue: 可以，只要有穩定的寬頻或 Wi‑Fi，就能在海外使用。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`

## What comes in the box
- English answer: The standard package includes the TV box, remote control, HDMI cable, power adapter, and quick-start leaflet.
- Mandarin cue: 標準配件包含主機、遙控器、HDMI 線、電源變壓器與快速入門說明。

## First-time setup steps
- English answer: Connect HDMI and power, choose a language, continue past the disclaimer, pair the remote, set time and date, join Wi‑Fi or Ethernet, then open Orz and install the needed apps.
- Mandarin cue: 先接上 HDMI 和電源，選語言，按 Continue，配對遙控器，設定時間與網路，再開啟 Orz 安裝需要的 App。
- Source: `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`

## Changing the system language
- English answer: On first boot, use the remote arrows to highlight the preferred language, then press OK and Next. The language can also be changed later in system settings.
- Mandarin cue: 首次開機時用遙控器選語言，按 OK 和 Next；之後也可在系統設定中更改。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`

## Remote not responding
- English answer: Replace the batteries first. Then hold VOL- and VOL+ together to re-pair the remote while staying close to the box.
- Mandarin cue: 先換電池，再同時按 VOL- 和 VOL+ 重新配對，配對時請靠近機身。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`, `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`

## Are the main apps pre-installed
- English answer: No. Open the Orz installer, enter 8989c.cc, and download Yogurt TV, Yogurt Kids, Cherry TV, Karaoke KTV, and other needed apps.
- Mandarin cue: 沒有預先安裝。請開啟 Orz，輸入 8989c.cc，再下載 Yogurt TV、Yogurt Kids、Cherry TV、Karaoke KTV 等 App。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`, `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`

- Mandarin cue: 可以安裝第三方 App，但請使用可信來源，並先確認支援 Android TV。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`, `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`

## Karaoke support
- English answer: Karaoke KTV is available on SVICLOUD 10P+ only. If karaoke matters, recommend 10P+ instead of 10S.
- Mandarin cue: Karaoke KTV 只有 10P+ 支援；如果卡拉 OK 很重要，建議選 10P+。

## Voice control
- English answer: Pair the remote first by holding VOL- and VOL+ together. Then press the microphone button and say the show or movie name. Voice control is not available on 10S.
- Mandarin cue: 先用 VOL- 和 VOL+ 配對遙控器，再按麥克風鍵說出節目名稱；10S 不支援語音功能。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`, `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`

## Subtitle speed and language
- English answer: Subtitle speed and multi-language subtitle options are available in playback settings.
- Mandarin cue: 播放設定裡可以調整字幕速度，也有多語字幕選項。

## Buffering or fuzzy streaming
- English answer: Restart the router, move the box closer to Wi‑Fi, or switch to Ethernet for better stability. Heavy traffic at home may require faster internet.
- Mandarin cue: 可先重啟路由器，把盒子移近 Wi‑Fi，或改用 Ethernet；如果家中同時上網設備很多，可能需要更快的網路。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`, `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`

## Orz installer site will not load
- English answer: Confirm the address is 8989c.cc. It may redirect to 6868c.cc. If it still fails, restart the box and make sure the internet connection is working.
- Mandarin cue: 請先確認輸入的是 8989c.cc，它可能會自動跳到 6868c.cc；如果還是不行，請重啟機器並確認網路正常。
- Source: `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq.md`, `docs/svicloudtvbox-faq-zh.md`, `docs/how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md`
