# SviCloud TV Box FAQ

A quick reference for common pre- and post-purchase questions. Tailored for the svicloudtvbox.us support and Guides pages.

## Device & Models

**Q: Which SviCloud model should I buy?**  
A: Choose SviCloud 10P+ if you want voice search and karaoke. SviCloud 10S delivers the same streaming catalog but does not support Karaoke KTV or the microphone-ready remote. Review the [SviCloud TV Box installation guide](how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md) if you need hardware setup help after choosing a model, and compare specs on the [SviCloud 10P+ vs 10S page](https://svicloudtvbox.us/compare/).

**Q: Does the TV box work outside of Asia?**  
A: Yes. The service is designed for international use—connect through any broadband or stable Wi-Fi network.

**Q: What is included in the box?**  
A: Every unit ships with the TV box, remote control, HDMI cable, power adapter, and quick-start leaflet.

## Setup & Activation

**Q: How do I turn on my SviCloud TV box?**  
A: Plug in the HDMI and power cables. The device boots up automatically when it receives power—the front LED confirms it is on. Full SviCloud installation steps live in our [setup guide](how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md).

**Q: How do I change the system language?**  
A: On first boot, highlight your preferred language with the remote arrows, press `OK`, then `Next`. You can revisit language settings later under the system preferences menu.

**Q: My remote is not responding. What do I do?**  
A: Replace the batteries, then hold `VOL-` and `VOL+` together to re-pair. Stay within a few feet of the box during pairing. This resolves most SviCloud troubleshooting requests tied to the voice remote.

## Apps & Content

**Q: Are SviCloud apps pre-installed?**  
A: No. Launch the `Orz` installer, visit `8989c.cc`, and download Yogurt TV, Yogurt Kids, Cherry TV, Karaoke KTV, and other essentials.

**Q: Can I install Netflix or other third-party apps?**  
A: Yes. SviCloud runs Android, so you can sideload popular streaming apps. Always download from trusted sources and confirm Android TV compatibility.

**Q: What content is available for families?**  
A: Yogurt Kids (also labeled LUCA Kids) offers educational shows, interactive games, and safe cartoons. We Fun / 閤家歡 karaoke adds family-friendly singalongs.

**Q: Is there adult content?**  
A: Cherry TV contains mature programming and is protected by a password. Users must opt in and enter the credential each time.

## Features & Limitations

**Q: Does every SviCloud unit support karaoke?**  
A: Only SviCloud 10P+. If karaoke is essential, pick this model over 10S.

**Q: How does voice control work?**  
A: After pairing the remote (hold `VOL-` + `VOL+`), press the mic button and speak the show or movie name. Voice control is unavailable on SviCloud 10S.

**Q: Can I adjust subtitle speed?**  
A: Yes. Subtitle pace and multi-language support are available in the playback settings menu.

## Troubleshooting & Support

**Q: Streaming looks fuzzy or keeps buffering.**  
A: Reboot your router, move the box closer to Wi-Fi, or connect via Ethernet. Heavy household traffic may require an upgraded broadband plan. For deeper SviCloud troubleshooting steps, reference the [installation guide](how-to-set-up-svicloud-tv-box.md).

**Q: The Orz installer site will not load.**  
A: Double-check the URL (`8989c.cc`, which redirects to `6868c.cc`). If the redirect fails, restart the box and confirm you have internet access.

**Q: How do I reach support?**  
A: Contact our customer-care team through the svicloudtvbox.us support portal or the email listed in your order confirmation. English and Traditional Chinese assistance is available.
