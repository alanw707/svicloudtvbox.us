# Facebook Black Friday 2025 Creative Brief

**Updated**: November 2025  
**Campaign**: “Up to 20% Off SVICLOUD Black Friday” Facebook/IG paid social  
**Primary CTA**: “Shop Black Friday” → `/black-friday?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=bf25-fb-20off`

---

## 1. Objectives & KPIs
- Drive qualified traffic that converts at CPA ≤ $40 and ROAS ≥ 4.5.
- Grow email opt-ins by 15% through landing-page capture (10% voucher).
- Educate prospects on concierge support + authorized dealer trust.
- Maximize urgency with “Sale ends Cyber Monday / limited units” narrative.

---

## 2. Audience Snapshot
| Tier | Audience | Key Insight | Desired Action |
|------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Prospecting | Lookalike (top 10% LTV) + interest stack (cord-cutters, IPTV, expats) | Need proof of legitimacy + clear value prop | Click-through to learn more |
| Warm Retargeting | Site engagers, video viewers, lead-ad responders, cart abandoners | Already aware; need urgency & reassurance | Complete purchase |
| Loyalty | Existing customers / CRM upload | Interested in gifting or upgrading hardware | Buy bundle, share offer |

---

## 3. Messaging Pillars
1. **Value + Savings**: “Save 20% on SVICLOUD 10P Plus bundles this Black Friday.”
2. **Confidence**: “Authorized dealer + lifetime concierge support.”
3. **Content Richness**: “4K streaming, 1,000+ live channels, zero subscription fees.”
4. **Urgency**: “Ends Cyber Monday” / “Limited units at 20% off.”

CTA Variants: “Unlock Deal,” “Shop Black Friday,” “Claim 20% Off.”

---

## 4. Deliverable Breakdown

### 4.1 15s Feature Montage Video
- **Format**: 1080x1080 & 1080x1920.
- **Structure**:
  1. Hook (0-3s): Animated “Up to 20% Off Black Friday” badge over hardware unboxing.
  2. Benefits (3-9s): Quick cuts showing interface, channel lineup, remote close-up; overlay text “4K streaming • 1,000+ channels • No monthly fees”.
  3. Assurance (9-12s): Concierge rep assisting customer; text “Authorized Dealer + Lifetime Support”.
  4. CTA (12-15s): Hero shot + countdown timer animation + “Shop now before the deal ends”.
- **VO/SFX**: Energetic underscore; optional VO script: “Only this weekend—save up to 20% on SVICLOUD. Get premium channels, lifetime support, and peace of mind.”
- **UTM Tag**: `utm_content=vid15`

### 4.2 10s Customer Testimonial Video
- **Format**: 1080x1080 & 9:16 cutdown.
- **Talent**: Existing user clip or voiceover quotes.
- **Structure**:
  1. Cold open with customer quote overlay: “Set-up was easy and the concierge team answered everything.”
  2. Show living-room environment + glimpsed channel switching.
  3. End card with 20% badge + copy: “Black Friday ends Cyber Monday.”
- **VO Script** (if needed): “I switched to SVICLOUD and instantly had my favorite channels back. Grab the Black Friday discount before it’s gone.”
- **UTM**: `utm_content=vid10-testimonial`

### 4.3 6s Offer Stinger
- **Use Case**: Bumpers, Reels, retargeting.
- **Storyboard**:
  1. Bold 20% typography with neon pulse effect.
  2. Hardware spin + overlay “Includes 3-month premium channel pack.”
  3. CTA flash “Tap to claim / Shop Black Friday”.
- **UTM**: `utm_content=stinger6`

### 4.4 Carousel Set (4 Cards)
| Card | Visual | Copy | Notes |
|------|--------|------|-------|
| 1 | Hero device on black backdrop with 20% seal | “Black Friday: Up to 20% Off SVICLOUD 10P Plus” | Include subtext “Authorized dealer advantage” |
| 2 | Channel grid collage | “1,000+ live channels, 4K-ready” | Add icons for sports/news/intl |
| 3 | Concierge/support imagery | “Lifetime concierge support included” | Mention peace of mind |
| 4 | Countdown timer graphic | “Ends Cyber Monday—Shop Now” | Button graphic “Claim Offer” |

UTM per card: `utm_content=carX` (X=1-4).

### 4.5 Stories/Reels Toolkit
- **Frames**: 3-frame sequence (Tease → Value → Swipe CTA).
- **Interactive Elements**: Countdown sticker to Cyber Monday deadline; poll (“Which feature matters more? Channels vs Support”).
- **Copy**: Keep under 7 words per frame; use high-contrast gradient backgrounds.
- **UTM**: `utm_content=storyseq`

### 4.6 Static Paid Social Variants
- **Concept A**: “Savings + Social Proof” – hero photo + 4.8⭐ badge, quote snippet.
- **Concept B**: “Feature Focus” – remote overlay, bullet list of features.
- **Concept C**: “Urgency Badge” – large “Final Hours” text + timer.
