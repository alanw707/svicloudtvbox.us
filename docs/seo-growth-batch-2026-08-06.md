## Cleanup Targets

Audit and improve older posts with:

- Meta descriptions that contain scraped navigation fragments.
- Titles that include "AI-assisted dry-run draft" or vague one-word titles.
- Image alts such as `hero-*.png`.
- Overlapping posts around 10P vs 10S, USA guide, local warehouse, and holiday promotions.
- Thin comparison posts that do not link to `/compare/`, product pages, support pages, and policy pages.
