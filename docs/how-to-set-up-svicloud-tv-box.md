# How to Set Up Your SviCloud TV Box

Welcome to the official step-by-step SviCloud TV Box installation guide for SviCloud (小雲) streaming media players. This walkthrough distills the English and Traditional Chinese product documentation into a single, easy-to-follow reference you can republish on the svicloudtvbox.us Guides hub.

## What You Can Do With SviCloud

- Android-based streaming box with fast, smooth playback for live TV, on-demand shows, and karaoke.
- Supports popular third-party services: Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, and more.
- Voice-enabled remote (10P+) for hands-free searches; multi-language UI with adjustable subtitles.

## SviCloud TV Box Installation Checklist

1. **Connect hardware**  
   Attach the HDMI cable to your TV and plug the power adapter into a wall outlet. The device powers on automatically (front LED lights up).

2. **Pick your language**  
   On first boot, use the remote’s arrow keys to highlight your preferred language and press `OK`, then `Next`.

3. **Accept the disclaimer**  
   Select `Continue` to proceed past the terms screen.

4. **Pair the remote for voice control**  
   Hold `VOL-` and `VOL+` together until the pairing confirmation appears. This completes the SviCloud 10P+ voice remote setup required for microphone searches on supported models.

5. **Set time and date**  
   Confirm the correct time zone so program guides display accurately.

6. **Join Wi-Fi (or Ethernet)**  
   Choose `Set up WiFi`, pick your network, and enter the password. Use a wired Ethernet connection if available for best stability.

7. **Install SviCloud apps**  
   - Launch the `Orz` app from the home screen.  
   - Enter installer URL `8989c.cc` (redirects to 6868c.cc).  
   - Download Yogurt TV, Yogurt Kids, Cherry TV, Karaoke KTV, and any other essentials. This completes the core SviCloud TV Box app installation workflow.

## After Setup

- Use the remote to explore the home screen and locate Live TV, VOD, and settings panels.
- Add additional Android streaming box apps as needed—just ensure they are sourced from trusted marketplaces.
- Visit settings at the bottom of the screen to personalize audio, display, and network preferences.

## Feature Notes & Model Differences

- **Karaoke KTV** is only available on SviCloud 10P+ models.
- **Voice control** requires SviCloud 10P+ (10S remotes do not support the mic pairing step).
- Multi-language support includes quick subtitle speed adjustments to match your viewing preference.

## Troubleshooting Tips

- If the remote fails to pair, remove the batteries, reinsert them, and repeat the `VOL-` + `VOL+` pairing process close to the box.
- Poor streaming quality? Restart the router, move closer to the Wi-Fi source, or switch to a wired Ethernet connection.
- Third-party app sideloading is allowed. Confirm app compatibility with Android TV before installing.
